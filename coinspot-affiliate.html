FAQ

Straight answers.

How much can I actually earn?

Commission starts at 30% of net trading fees for the first 12 months after a member verifies, then 15% for months 12–24 and 5% for months 24–36. CoinSpot states some of its most successful affiliates earn over $5,000 per month.*

When do I get paid?

Payments are issued by the 15th of each month, based on fees collected from members you referred — for three years from each member's verification.

What counts toward my commission?

Your commission is a percentage of the net fees CoinSpot collects from your referred members: 1% on AUD instant Buy/Sell (Bitcoin), 1% on other coins, and 0.5% on crypto-to-crypto trades.

Is there a limit to how many people I can refer?

No — more verified members simply means a larger monthly commission pool. Higher-performing members can also be contacted about incentives above the standard structure.

Are SMSF accounts eligible?

No. SMSF (self-managed super) accounts can't participate due to legislative restrictions — you'll need a separate individual or business account to join.

Can the terms change?

CoinSpot notes that fees and commissions are subject to change, and that they may offer incentives above this structure for high performers. Always check the current Affiliate Terms & Conditions.
